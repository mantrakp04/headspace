# Headspace 1.0.0

The original Headspace player skin, revived for macOS.

- Stream Spotify inside the app and browse playlists, albums, liked songs, and podcasts.
- Open the sliding panels and choose from seven visualizations.
- Keep the player above your windows, resize it, or tuck it into a corner.
- Receive signed updates through the Headspace menu and automatic update checks.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later, and Spotify Premium. Your Spotify developer account must permit access to the configured Spotify app; development-mode restrictions may limit who can sign in.

Open **Headspace.dmg**, drag **Headspace** into **Applications**, and launch it. Connect Spotify from the library's account screen.
