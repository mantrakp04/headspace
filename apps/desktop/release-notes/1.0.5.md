# Headspace 1.0.5

- Add Apple Music as a second music service. Choose **Apple Music** at the top of the account panel to switch; Headspace remembers the choice. An Apple Music subscription is required.
- Browse Apple Music playlists, albums, artists, songs, and recent listening, add songs to your library, and add songs to playlists you can edit.
- Fix playback races when switching tracks quickly, repeated songs in queues, and playlists sharing IDs.
- Ignore late errors and playback readings from the previously selected service after switching.
